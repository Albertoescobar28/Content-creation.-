# My Content Pillars (draft)

Following P81 (pillars = brand-level themes, not sub-topic buckets)
and P77 (T-shaped model: ~80% on-pillar, ~20% flavor). Four pillars
proposed, drawn directly from the audience research and story arc
already on file.

## Pillar 1 — The Shift-Work Fat Loss System
The mechanism: how fat loss actually works around early starts, late
shifts, overtime, and no control over food at the job. Meal timing,
the "overtime backup," cooler/lunchbox systems, training around
fatigue instead of pretending the job doesn't exist. This is the
core "how" pillar — most bottom-of-funnel content lives here.

## Pillar 2 — Reframes and Myth-Busting
Attacking the specific beliefs blue-collar men repeat to themselves
("my job is already exercise," "I barely eat, so why am I still fat,"
"overtime ruins everything"). Contrarian, opinionated, un-nuanced per
voice-rules.md. Strong top-of-funnel pillar because these beliefs are
near-universal in the audience.

## Pillar 3 — Proof / Story
Personal journey content pulled from story-arc.md: 215 lbs and the
crash-diet year, the DoorDash winter, the unused gym membership, "people
started asking if I was a trainer," betting $10,000 on himself. Builds
trust and follower quality (P16) without needing client data.

## Pillar 4 — Client Results and Case Studies
Real transformations and before/after systems from coaching clients.
Needs your input: which client stories can you actually share, and do
you have before/afters or testimonials on file? This pillar stays
empty until you confirm what's usable.
