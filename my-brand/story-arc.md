# My Story Arc

Master reference for proof content, story-pool posts, email sequences,
and origin-story content. This is the complete picture so pieces can be
chosen deliberately.

## Timeline

**2012 — The Divorce.** Parents divorced abruptly; father drank too
much, mother couldn't handle it, he left.

**2018 — Losing My Father.** Father passed suddenly from a heart
attack. No warning.

**2020 — Moving to the US.** Moved to the US two weeks before COVID
lockdown. No English, no friends, world shut down almost immediately.
DoorDash was the entire food system. 160 lbs, skinny-fat, new country,
global shutdown, only family as a constant. Started construction work
in October 2020; brutal winter, roofing in the snow.

**2021 — The Gym Membership That Sat Unused.** Joined Anytime Fitness,
went once. Believed he had to go 5-6 days/week for it to "count," and
that drinking meant he'd never lose weight anyway, so he didn't try.
Worked construction all year, sometimes driving over an hour to job
sites; the grind taught him to function on brutal schedules.

**2022 — Gained Weight, But Also Gained Muscle.** Gained ~30 lbs but
also built real muscle from construction work. Bought a one-year gym
membership, started with mostly cardio (didn't know what else to do).
Watched YouTube workouts, touched dumbbells for the first time. Quit
construction by year end ($5,000 in debt, wasn't paying enough), moved
into manufacturing shift jobs with overtime. Drinking heavily since
2019 (four years in by this point).

**2023 — 215 lbs, Heaviest.** 55 lbs gained over three years without
realizing the accumulation. Turning point: cut off the people he was
drinking with, went all-in (gym 7 days/week, heavy lifting + cardio,
fasting, low-carb, ~1,500 cal for a long stretch). Lost 45 lbs, dropped
to 170, but lost a lot of muscle, ended up looking like he hadn't
trained. A relationship ended this year too; shame about debt was
hidden underneath a "health-focused" excuse.

**2024 — Correcting the Overcorrection.** Fixed the prior year's
mistakes: ate more, tracked calories properly, hit 180-200g protein
daily, 5-day training split, walked 10-12k steps, cut cardio entirely.
Gained real muscle, got leaner, reached the physique he'd been chasing
since 2022. People at work started treating him differently, asking
his opinion. Started therapy. People at work/gym started asking if he
was a trainer, coaching started almost by accident, three months after
the breakup. Used coaching income to quietly pay down the $5,000 debt,
fix credit, build first real savings. Built first coaching systems on
Google Sheets, got first paying clients in December.

**2025 — Going Deeper, Building the Business, Learning What Doesn't
Work.** Studied training/coaching more seriously; faith grew stronger.
Ran a full intentional bulk-to-190-then-cut-to-170 cycle, not guessing,
proving the process. Built out Instagram presence, mostly failed at
content that didn't work (recipe videos, generic workout content),
burned out chasing virality instead of serving existing clients.
Around March-May, stepped back from content to refocus on people
already in the program, wrote the four habits (sleep, nutrition,
strength training, daily movement) that became the foundation of
everything taught now. Moved out of parents' house, living below his
means, saving, cutting out old drinking circle, going deeper into
coaching, learning, podcasting, self-development, in parallel with
therapy.

**2026 — Betting On Myself.** In July, quit the full-time job. No
transition period, no backup contract, no guaranteed paycheck. Two
years earlier: $5,000 in debt, overdrawn account, full-time job, still
couldn't afford to take someone out, too ashamed to say it out loud.
This year: $20,000 saved, tracked every dollar the same way he taught
clients to track every meal. Took $10,000 of it and invested in
mentorship, the riskiest move since moving to the country with
nothing. Framing: building something big enough to eventually retire
his mom, so her sacrifices mean more than survival.

## Content angles pulled from this arc

Each is a complete Reel on its own:
- The DoorDash winter: skinny-fat, no English, no friends, locked down in a new country
- Roofing in the snow: the version of "hard work" nobody sees on Instagram
- The gym membership he paid for and never used, and the two lies that kept him away
- 215 lbs, the year everything caught up with him at once
- Losing 45 lbs and still looking soft: what nobody tells you about crash dieting
- The year he had to un-learn his own overcorrection
- "People started asking if I was a trainer": how coaching found him, not the other way around
- $10,000 on himself: betting everything on the business after two years of proof

## What's public vs private

Some of this (father's passing, the divorce, the breakup, the
debt/shame) is heavy. Decide deliberately which chapters get told in
full, which get referenced briefly, and which stay as background
context that informs the coaching without becoming content. Not
everything needs to go public.
