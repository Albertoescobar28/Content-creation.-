# My Voice

This file needs two things I can't write for you: your **voice
signature** and 2+ **voice samples** of content you actually wrote or
said on camera. `voice-rules.md` (the agency-wide rules) already covers
things like no em-dashes and fifth-grade reading level — this file is
about what makes *your* voice different from every other coach
following those same rules.

## What to send me

1. **2-5 real samples** — actual captions, hooks, or scripts you've
   posted (or even just paste one from a video you've already filmed).
   The rawer the better; don't clean them up first.
2. **A rough voice signature** — a few sentences on how you'd describe
   your own tone if you had to explain it to someone else. If that's
   hard to do cold, answer these instead and I'll draft the signature
   with you:
   - Do you swear on camera? How much slang/casual language?
   - Short punchy sentences, or do you let yourself run longer when
     something matters?
   - How much of your own story do you bring into fat-loss/training
     content versus keeping it purely tactical?
   - Any phrases, words, or openers you catch yourself using a lot?
   - Anything you never want to sound like (a specific creator, a
     "guru" tone, overly polished, etc.)?

## A starting observation (not a substitute for the above)

`story-arc.md` is written in your own words, first person, and it
already shows some real signal: short, concrete, declarative sentences;
specific numbers over vague claims ($5,000 in debt, 215 lbs, 45 lbs
lost); willingness to state hard things plainly (the drinking, the
debt, the shame) without over-explaining or softening them. That lines
up well with `voice-rules.md`'s "falsifiable and tangible" and
"definitive stance, avoid hedging" rules. Treat this as a hypothesis,
not your actual voice.md — it's not built from content you wrote *to
an audience*, just from a private reference document.

Once you send real samples, I'll turn this into a proper signature +
sample bank and `/ideate` will stop warning about it.
